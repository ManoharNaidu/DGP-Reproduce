# One statistics standard for E2-E16

Every paired comparison of the DGP follow-up experiments, re-tested the same way (`scripts/stats_standard.py`). Test set, AmazonVideo (E16: YelpChi), seeds 0-4. Δ = mean paired difference arm − reference in pp; 'better' = seeds where the arm is higher; CI = 95% t-interval of the mean seed difference; p = two-sided paired t over seeds (df 4). Holm (family) corrects over 3 metrics × the comparisons of one experiment; Holm (global) over all 105 tests. Bold = Holm (family) < 0.05. Seed-level only: test-set sampling noise is not included (see each study's bootstrap CIs).
